Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY

Benefits: Health cover of Rs 5 lakh per family per year for secondary and tertiary hospitalisation at empanelled public and private hospitals. Cashless and paperless treatment.

Eligibility: Families identified from the SECC database and listed occupational categories. No limit on family size or age.

Documents required: Aadhaar card, ration card, mobile number, Ayushman card (generated after verification).

How to apply: 1. Check eligibility at mera.pmjay.gov.in or call 14555. 2. Visit the nearest CSC or empanelled hospital Ayushman Mitra with Aadhaar and ration card. 3. Complete verification and get the Ayushman card. 4. Use the card for cashless treatment.
