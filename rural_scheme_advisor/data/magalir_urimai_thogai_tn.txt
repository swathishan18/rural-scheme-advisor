Kalaignar Magalir Urimai Thogai (Tamil Nadu)

Benefits: Monthly assistance of Rs 1,000 to women heads of eligible families in Tamil Nadu, credited directly to the bank account.

Eligibility: Woman head of the family holding a ration card in Tamil Nadu, with annual family income within the limit set by the state government. Certain categories such as government employees and income tax payers are excluded.

Documents required: Ration card, Aadhaar, bank passbook, mobile number.

How to apply: 1. Collect the application form from the ration shop / special camp. 2. Fill details and attach documents. 3. Submit at the registration camp. 4. Verification is done by officials and approved applicants receive the amount monthly.
