MGNREGA (Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act)

Benefits: Guarantees at least 100 days of wage employment per financial year to every rural household whose adult members volunteer to do unskilled manual work. Wages are paid directly to the bank/post office account.

Eligibility: Any adult member of a rural household willing to do unskilled manual work.

Documents required: Job card (applied through Gram Panchayat), Aadhaar, bank or post office account, passport size photo.

How to apply: 1. Apply for a Job Card at the Gram Panchayat office with a photo and ID. 2. Gram Panchayat issues the Job Card after verification. 3. Submit a written or oral application for work. 4. Work should be provided within 15 days of application.
