PMAY-G (Pradhan Mantri Awaas Yojana - Gramin)

Benefits: Financial assistance to build a pucca house with basic amenities. Assistance of Rs 1.20 lakh in plain areas and Rs 1.30 lakh in hilly/difficult areas. Additional support for toilet construction under Swachh Bharat Mission and 90/95 days of MGNREGA wages for construction.

Eligibility: Houseless families and families living in kutcha or dilapidated houses, identified through the SECC data and verified by Gram Sabha.

Documents required: Aadhaar, job card number, bank account details, SECC/awaas plus listing proof.

How to apply: 1. Check your name in the beneficiary list at the Gram Panchayat. 2. Contact the Block Development Office (BDO) if listed. 3. Submit Aadhaar and bank details. 4. Construction is monitored using geo-tagged photos; instalments are released in stages.
